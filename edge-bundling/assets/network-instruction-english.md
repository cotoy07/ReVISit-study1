## Research Overview

This survey is conducted as part of a research project evaluating the readability of network diagrams.

A network diagram represents connections between people or objects using "points (nodes)" and "lines connecting them (edges)." Although network diagrams are widely used across various fields to understand data relationships, a high volume of lines can make the diagram cluttered and difficult to interpret. To address this issue, a rendering technique known as "edge bundling" is often applied to bundle multiple lines together, improving visual clarity.

The purpose of this study is to analyze the specific conditions—such as edge bundling methods, network types, density, and scale—under which high readability is achieved, and to identify the optimal combinations of these factors.

During the survey, several network diagrams will be displayed in sequence. For each diagram, please evaluate the following three aspects:

* **Ease of Information Acquisition:** Is it easy to grasp the relationships and the overall structure?
* **Visual Clarity:** Are the nodes and edges easily distinguishable?
* **Aesthetic Appeal:** Do you find the diagram visually pleasing?
